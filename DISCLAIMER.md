# Disclaimer

BedsideBench is provided only for evaluating AI systems. It is not medical
advice and must not be used to diagnose, treat, or otherwise guide care.

Some cases deliberately contain false premises, fictitious entities, or unsafe
actions. These are evaluation probes. Model responses generated from the cases
may also be incorrect or unsafe.

The cases are synthetic or de-identified constructions, not patient records.
They are not intended to contain protected health information. Report any
potentially identifiable content through the repository issue tracker so it can
be reviewed and removed.

Rubric criteria reflect clinical judgment and the available standard of care at
the time they were written. Guidelines and evidence change. A benchmark score
does not establish that a model is safe or suitable for clinical use.

copyright holders are not liable for claims, damages, or other liability arising
from its use.
